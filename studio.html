Memento Studio 3D memento preview tool · version {{ appVersion }}
What it is Memento Studio turns a customer’s plate design into a photo-real 3D preview of the finished memento — the printed acrylic plate standing in a real SAI KIRAN Wood Works base, lit and placed on a backdrop — before anything is cut or printed.
What it is used for Showing customers how their memento will look and getting approval on WhatsApp, checking that a design fits the chosen base, making product photos for the shop, catalogue and social media, and exporting 3D models of finished pieces.
How it works
1 Pick a baseChoose a base variant or upload its STEP, GLB, OBJ or STL file.
2 Add the plate designUpload the artwork. The tool cuts out the shape and sizes it from the file’s DPI.
3 Set the sceneFit the plate to the slot, choose finishes, light, backdrop and floor.
4 DownloadSave a JPG, PNG or WebP image, with privacy blur and watermark if needed, or a 3D GLB model.
Works offline, stays private Everything runs on this device. Customer artwork is never uploaded, and your settings are remembered for next time.
